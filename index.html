Builder Tycoon

Build solid. Grow your name.

An old pickup truck, a little savings and one rule: the building you put up should outlive you. Strike land-for-units deals with landowners, get your permits and raise your towers floor by floor, from Istanbul all the way to Monaco.

  • Land-for-units dealsSit down with Auntie Fatma and friends to trade plots for apartments.
  • Honest building winsCut corners and the inspector brings fines, demolitions and sealed sites.
  • 18 citiesGrow from one plot on Gül Street to skylines from Baku to Tokyo.
  • Weekly seasonsClimb your country and city leaderboards for rewards.

  • Plays offlineNo account needed. Night and day themes.
